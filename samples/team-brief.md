# A better Monday

A small plan for making shared work feel simpler.

## What matters

- **Clarity:** one place for the current draft.
- **Trust:** changes save and access is intentional.
- **Momentum:** fewer steps between a thought and a teammate.

## This week's plan

1. Write a first draft.
2. Invite a teammate to review.
3. Turn the feedback into a clear next step.

> Good work starts with a little room to think.
